### B. Viewer (`src/viewer/**`)
- Default floor and ceiling use their finishes (colour / texture × colour) over the existing floor
  bounds. Floor/ceiling UVs are **world-aligned**: u = worldX / tileSize, v = worldZ / tileSize
  (orientation such that textures aren't mirrored when seen from above for floors / from below for ceilings).
- Areas: a floor area renders as a horizontal quad over its rect just above the floor; a ceiling area
  just below the ceiling (ceiling areas only when `settings.ceiling` is true). Stacking follows array
  order without z-fighting (use `polygonOffset` with increasing units per layer and/or tiny y offsets
  ≤ 1 mm per layer — your choice, explain it). Same world-aligned UVs so adjacent areas with the same
  texture are seamless.
- Draw calls stay bounded: merge all area quads that share a finish *and* layer index band into one
  mesh where correct stacking allows (at minimum: merge per finish when areas of that finish don't
  overlap areas of other finishes; otherwise per area is acceptable). State what you did and the
  worst-case extra draw calls; it must not scale with wall/work count. Textures loaded once per media
  item (shared with walls). Floor bounds, collision and teleport are unchanged (areas don't affect movement).
- Missing texture → colour + warning, like walls.

### C. Editor (`src/editor/**`)
- **Layers**: a mode switch "Build" (walls, works, zones, spawn — today's behaviour) vs "Areas".
  In Areas mode: walls/works/zones are drawn dimmed and are not hit-testable; areas are drawn with
  their finish colour (semi-opaque, texture thumbnail pattern optional) and outline, and are the only
  selectable/draggable things. In Build mode areas are faint and not hit-testable. Keyboard shortcut
  for the switch (e.g. `A`) and visible indicator.
- Area tools (Areas mode): draw rectangle (drag), select, move, resize via handles, delete,
  duplicate. Snapping: grid, plus edges snap to wall centre lines (horizontal/vertical walls) within
  ~0.3 m.
- **Fill room**: click inside a room → create an area whose rect is the largest axis-aligned rectangle
  bounded by the nearest walls around the click: cast rays left/right/up/down from the click to the
  nearest wall segment (ignoring walls the ray misses, e.g. through doorway gaps the ray continues),
  rect edges at those walls' centre lines. If any direction hits nothing within the floor bounds,
  show a notice and create nothing. Pure, tested function (closed rectangular room, room with a
  doorway gap on the ray path → ray continues to the next wall, open side → null, click inside a wall → null).
- Ordering: "Bring forward / Send backward / To front / To back" in the area inspector (array order).
- Area inspector: name, rect (x, y, w, h), "Floor" toggle + Finish fields, "Ceiling" toggle + Finish
  fields (reuse the wall finish editor component); at least one must stay enabled.
- Settings panel: floor finish and ceiling finish editors next to the wall finish.
- Undo/redo covers all area operations (drags as one step).
- Warnings: area outside floor bounds is fine (no warning); area with missing texture → warning.
