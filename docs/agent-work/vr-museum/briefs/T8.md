## Goal
A sky visible beyond walls and through ceiling openings, and areas that remove the ceiling
("open sky") so visitors can be "outside". Ceiling rendering becomes a flat decomposition, so
ceiling areas no longer need vertical stacking offsets.

### B. Viewer (`src/viewer/**`)
- **Sky**: gradient = a large inward-facing sphere or `scene.background` shader/texture with smooth
  top→horizon→bottom blend (horizon at eye-level-ish, i.e. the horizontal plane); must render in
  WebXR stereo (no screen-space tricks that break per-eye). Panorama = equirectangular texture
  (sRGB, max 4096 px longest side) on an inward sphere or `scene.background` with equirect mapping,
  rotated by `rotationDeg` (clockwise from above, 0 = image centre toward plan -y). The sky must not
  move with translation (follows the camera position or is effectively at infinity), and must not
  be affected by fog/lighting. Missing panorama → default gradient + warning. Remove the flat grey
  `scene.background` colour.
- **Ceiling decomposition**: replace ceiling area layering with a pure function (in
  `src/viewer/surfaces.ts` or a new module) that takes the ceiling bounds rect, the base ceiling
  finish and the areas (in order), splits the bounds into grid cells from all relevant area edges,
  assigns each cell the result of the **top-most** area covering it that has `ceiling` or `openSky`
  (else the base finish), and returns cells grouped by result: finish groups (merge adjacent cells
  into larger rects where cheap — at least merge along rows) plus "open" (no geometry). All
  ceiling quads sit at exactly wallHeight (no offsets). Keep world-aligned UVs and one merged mesh
  per finish. Tests: no areas → one base rect; one open area in the middle → base covers bounds
  minus the hole (total area check); ceiling area over an open area (later wins) → re-covered;
  open area over a ceiling area → hole; floor-only areas ignored; touching/overlapping edges; many
  areas don't explode (e.g. 30 areas → cells bounded, rects merged).
- Floors keep the T7 overlap-level approach. `settings.ceiling === false` still disables all ceilings.
- Lighting stays as is (no per-area light). If open areas look too dark/bright, don't tune globally — just report.
