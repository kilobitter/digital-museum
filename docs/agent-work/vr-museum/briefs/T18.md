### Ceiling extent helper (replaces rect-based limiting)
The ceiling is cut into a grid of rects along EVERY area edge, also between rects of equal height. So limiting a strip by `exitDistance` from its own rect (line 186) has a flaw: a strip
shrinks beside an area edge even when the ceiling simply continues at the same height. Add a helper that walks through neighbouring rects:
```ts
/** How far from plan point q along unit direction dir (up to max) the ceiling stays at `height`. 0 when q itself has no ceiling at that height. */
function ceilingExtent(ceilings: readonly CeilingGroup[], q: Vec2, dir: Vec2, height: number, max: number): number;
```
Height match within 1e-6. Rects are half-open at their edges: a point exactly on an edge between two equal-height rects must count as ceiling. Precision to about 1 cm is enough; it may step or compute exactly.
- The ceiling strip's `cw` (line 186) and the header's `cw` (line 258) use it. Keep the existing limit behaviour where the height really changes or the ceiling ends: the T16 tests must keep passing unchanged.
- Add a test: wall 0.15 m from an area edge where both sides of that edge have the SAME height: the strip keeps full `CEILING_WIDTH`.

### Fans
Generalise `Accumulator.fan` for ceilings, keeping the floor's result byte-identical (the existing floor tests must pass unchanged; compare the floor vertices before and after): a facing normal (`[0, -1, 0]` on a ceiling), and a radius per ray, so that a fan stops at the edge of its ceiling through `ceilingExtent`. A ray of radius 0 is allowed and produces no visible triangles.

Ceiling fans lie at `height - GAP`, the same plane as the ceiling strips.

**Exposed convex corner.** In the existing corner loop, after the floor fan. The height comes from the ceiling at a point just outside the corner along the wedge's bisector, with both walls reaching that height (`g.height <= p.top` for the pieces at the corner). No ceiling there: no fan.

**Free end.** The floor uses one half-disc, but the two sides of a wall may have different ceilings, so build the ceiling version per side: one quarter from `corner(side)` to the end's midpoint, sweeping from that side's normal to the end direction, at that side's ceiling height, sampled just outside the wall near the end, as the strips do. Likewise one jamb strip per side on the cap face, from `corner(side)` to the midpoint, hanging `CEILING_WIDTH` from that height, clamped to the piece's top. Mind that the current free-end loop runs once per end (`if (side === -1) continue`), so do not nest side loops by accident.

**Header exclusion.** A free end is continued by a header when a `headers` entry has the same wall or a parallel one and one of its `step.a`/`step.b` lies within `T` of the end point. Skip its ceiling fan and jamb strip. Test both: a door between rooms of different height, and a door between rooms of equal height (no header: gets fans).
