## Changes
- src/viewer/contactShadows.ts
  - `ceilingExtent(ceilings, q, dir, height, max)`: walks through neighbouring ceiling rects of equal height (1e-6), nudging 1e-5 past each edge so half-open rects count as ceiling; returns 0 when q has no ceiling at that height. `exitDistance` now takes plan Vec2s.
  - Ceiling strip `cw` and header `cw` use `ceilingExtent` (the old rect-limited behaviour is kept where height changes or the ceiling ends; a strip no longer shrinks beside an area edge where the ceiling simply continues).
  - `Accumulator.fan` generalised: facing normal `n` (default up), radius either a number or a per-ray function, optional segment count. The floor call is unchanged and its output is byte-identical.
  - Exposed convex corner: after the floor fan, a ceiling fan at `height - GAP` over the outer wedge. Height is the ceiling at the chord midpoint plus 1 cm along the bisector. Both walls must reach it (`c.top` added to `cornerEnd`). Radius per ray comes from `ceilingExtent`, so it stops at its ceiling's edge.
  - Free end: per side (inner `sd` loop inside the existing once-per-end block), one quarter fan from `corner(sd)` to the end midpoint, sweeping from that side's normal to the end direction (3 segments, so its rays line up with the floor half-disc). Also one jamb strip on the cap face hanging `CEILING_WIDTH` from that side's ceiling (limited to the height). Each side samples its own ceiling just outside the wall near the end, and nothing is drawn if the ceiling is above `p.top`.
  - Header exclusion: a free end is skipped (fans and jambs) when a `headers` entry has the same or a parallel wall and a `step.a`/`step.b` within T of the end centre.
  - One decision beyond the brief: ceiling strips now run to `s0/s1`, the same GAP-extended limits the floor strips use at capped ends. Without it a 1.5 mm gap between a ceiling strip and the new fan showed as a 1 px light hairline (measured +8 levels; after the change it is gone). It is in the ceiling-strip loop only and changes strips by 1.5 mm at capped ends.
- src/viewer/contactShadows.test.ts: 17 new tests plus one edited assertion (below).
  - Corner fans: axis, reversed, diagonal, diagonal reversed. Exact radius and alpha at the axis corner, nothing on the inner side.
  - No ceiling (`ceilings: []`), open sky at a corner and at a free end.
  - An area edge 0.15 m beyond the corner (tall, sky, equal height with a different finish).
  - A strip beside an equal-height area edge keeps its full width.
  - Free-end quarter fans and jambs in both directions, with their facing direction.
  - Different heights on the two sides of a free end.
  - A ceiling above the piece top gets nothing.
  - Header exclusion (different heights), and equal-height door ends get fans.
  - Floor vertices identical to 5562fe9: 444 floor triangles and an FNV hash taken from the old code on a museum with corners, free ends, a diagonal, a T, stacked areas and a tall room (`FLOOR_COUNT`/`FLOOR_HASH` at the file's end).
  - Everything finite.
  - EDITED existing assertion in 'door header: contact on the header face...': "nothing in the gap without headers" now checks only the middle of the doorway (|x-3.5|<0.1). Free ends now legitimately have ceiling fans reaching 0.3 m into the gap, so the old whole-gap check could not hold. The T16 limiting tests are untouched and pass.
