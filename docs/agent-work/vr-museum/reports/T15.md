## Changes
- src/viewer/surfaces.ts: new exported UncoveredStep (CeilingStep plus optional wall). uncoveredSteps records each parallel covering wall's drawn range [lo,hi] (min/max over its spans) and sets piece.wall: first a wall with hi ~ f0, else a wall with lo ~ f1 (1e-6). The key is left out when none touches.
- src/viewer/wallGeometry.ts: new doorHeaderGeometry(step, wall, thickness, tileSize, drawn). It builds a face flush with the wall's face on the tall side, with the wall's own across coordinate, and an underside at step.bottom running back to the step line, skipped when under 1e-4 m. Winding is checked against the wanted normal. UVs continue the wall box (+z or opposite u formula chosen by n . perp; v = y / tileSize on the face). The surfaces import is type-only.
- src/viewer/scene.ts: local wallEntry(finish) helper shared by walls and headers (same merge key). Steps with a wall go into a headers list. Headers are added to the wall entry before merging, using effectiveFinish, its tileSize and drawn from wallPieceMap. The gradient uses applyWallGradient(geo, 0, ...) with the wall's max piece top, with a comment. Steps with no wall are unchanged (stepQuad, ceiling material).
- README.md: replaced the strip sentence as specified.
- Tests added: src/viewer/surfaces.test.ts (6 uncoveredSteps cases) and src/viewer/wallGeometry.test.ts (doorHeaderGeometry: 4 normals x both wall directions x 3 offsets for face plane, y range, winding, underside, u/v against a real wallGeometry; plus merge and gradient colour match).
